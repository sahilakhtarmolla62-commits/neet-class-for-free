Help & Notes
Will my videos stay after refresh?
Yes. This site saves your videos in your browser storage (IndexedDB). Refresh/back won’t remove them.
Can my friends see my videos by opening this site link?
Not automatically. Without a server, your videos live only on your device. To share, use Export Vault to create a .zip and send it to friends. They can Import it here.
Is there a size limit?
Browsers have storage limits. If saving fails, export a backup and free space or use fewer/lower-size videos.
Best practice
  • Export your Vault regularly for backup.
  • Keep originals elsewhere if the videos are important.
  • Use clear names (date + event) for easy searching.
